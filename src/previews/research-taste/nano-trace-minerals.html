Nano trace minerals

Nano-Activated Functionalised Trace Minerals

Request technical dossier ↗

The next frontier in bioavailability. Breaking minerals down to nanoscale and tagging each particle with bioactives aims to get more of the mineral absorbed, so less needs to go into the feed.

How it works ↓Product connections ↓

See the mechanism

How it works

The same amount of mineral, split finer and tagged, presents far more of itself to the gut.

Activate to nanoscale+

A mineral particle is broken down into many nanoscale particles. The amount of mineral stays the same, but its surface area multiplies, and surface is where dissolving and absorption begin.

Functionalise the surface+

Bioactive tags bind to each particle. They keep the particles from clumping back together and bring bioactivity of their own.

Absorb at a lower dose+

Well-spread particles with more surface present more mineral to the gut wall, so the same result can come from a lower inclusion rate.

Why it matters

  • Lower doses

    Aims to meet mineral needs with less mineral in the feed.

  • Less left over

    Minerals that aren't absorbed pass out in manure. Better absorption leaves less behind.

  • Two jobs in one

    Tags add bioactivity on top of the mineral's own role.
