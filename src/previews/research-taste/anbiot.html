How it works

Three stages take a plant active from the field to a feed additive that works in the animal.

Start with the plant+

We draw phytochemicals from botanicals used in Ayurveda, along with agri by-products. In their raw form these actives vary from batch to batch, and many are hard for animals to use.

Transform them+

A proprietary nutrient medium drives a natural bio-transformation under mild conditions, in line with green chemistry. It converts the actives into forms the animal can use.

Protect and deliver+

Multi-layer encapsulation stabilises the result. It protects the active through storage and feed processing, then releases it where it works in the animal.
