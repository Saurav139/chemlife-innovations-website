Nature-identical molecules

Synthesis of Nature-Identical Bioactive Molecules

Request technical dossier ↗

The amount of an active compound in a herb changes with soil, season and harvest. We identify the molecule that does the work and make a nature-identical version through green synthesis, so every batch carries the same dose.

How it works

Nature-identical means the molecule is chemically the same as the one the plant makes. Only its source changes.

Find the active+

Start from a herb with a known benefit and identify the molecule responsible for it.

Make it greener+

Produce that same molecule through green synthesis and biotechnology routes, instead of extracting it from large volumes of plant material.

Match and standardise+

Confirm the molecule is identical to the one in the plant, then dose it to a fixed specification in every batch.
