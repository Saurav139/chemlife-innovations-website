5 research platforms

ANBioT

ANBioT is the technology behind our nature-driven range. It pairs Ayurvedic plant knowledge with modern bio-transformation, turning plant actives into consistent, feed-ready ingredients.

Green chelation

We bind trace minerals to protein from silkworm pupae, a by-product of India's silk industry. A gentle ANBioT process does the bonding, and the result is MinBioZen, a bio-trace mineral for animal feed.

Microencapsulation

A cow's rumen breaks down much of what it eats before the nutrients reach the intestine. Our multi-layer microencapsulation sets where a nutrient is released: slowly in the rumen, or protected past it to the intestine.

Nano trace minerals

The next frontier in bioavailability. Breaking minerals down to nanoscale and tagging each particle with bioactives aims to get more of the mineral absorbed, so less needs to go into the feed.

Nature-identical molecules

The amount of an active compound in a herb changes with soil, season and harvest. We identify the molecule that does the work and make a nature-identical version through green synthesis, so every batch carries the same dose.
