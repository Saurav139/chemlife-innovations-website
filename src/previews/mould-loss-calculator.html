Not counted here

This only counts spoiled feed. As the article explains, mould also costs farms in ways that are harder to put a number on, so the real saving is likely higher:

  • Lost nutrients in feed that still gets eaten
  • Lower feed intake and slower growth, and poorer feed conversion (FCR)
  • Fewer eggs
  • Weaker immunity, more disease, higher mortality and vet bills
  • Mycotoxin residues in meat and eggs

How it works

Feed lost = feed per year × share lost to mould × feed price.

Cost of prevention = feed per year × cost per tonne.

Saving = feed lost × share prevention stops − cost of prevention.

An estimate for planning, not a guarantee. Mould risk depends on climate, storage, moisture and feed ingredients. Talk to our nutritionists about your farm.
