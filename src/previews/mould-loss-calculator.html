How it works

  • Storage loss is the feed you make, times the share lost to mould, times its value.
  • Flock loss is the profit on the birds your feed grows with clean feed, minus their profit with this feed. Mycotoxins mean more deaths, a worse FCR and lighter birds.
  • Treating costs the same on every tonne. The mould inhibitor stops all of the storage loss. The toxin binder prevents part of the flock loss.
  • Rejecting costs the part of the feed's value you don't get back. The birds then eat clean feed. The storage loss stays.

The flock loss falls on whoever grows the birds: you, or the customers who judge your feed by it. This is an estimate for planning, not a prediction. Transport and delay on rejected feed are not counted.

Where the figures come from
  • Storage loss and treatment cost: 10 to 15% lost in unprotected stores and ₹500 to ₹1,000 a tonne, from our article The Economics of Prevention. Placeholder The 5% starting figure for a mill is our guess.
  • Mycotoxins, full effect: FCR +0.12, deaths +2.0 points, weight −217 g, applied here as 9% of live weight (Weaver 2022, 25 trials).
  • Mycotoxin level: no effect under 20 ppb, half at 20 to 200 ppb, full above 200 ppb. Our reading of aflatoxin dose trials.
  • Hot season: FCR +0.17, weight −6% (Liu 2020). To check against the full paper. No published figure for deaths, so none is added.
  • Cold season: deaths +7.6 points from ascites (Ipek & Sahan 2006, a severe-cold trial). No published percentage for FCR, so none is added.
  • Acidifier: FCR 3% better (Polycarpo 2017 reports up to 5.7% under disease pressure).
  • Bird figures: FCR 1.80 and 95.3% survival from an Indian field study; dressing yield 67% at about 2.2 kg (IJAS 2024).
  • Placeholder Feed value, other costs per bird, how much flock loss treatment prevents and the money back on rejected feed are our starting guesses, for ChemLife to replace.

Effects are added together, which understates flocks hit by heat and mycotoxins at once. Vaccination and antibiotic growth promoters are left out: vaccines protect against deaths only when the disease is present, and antibiotic growth promoters give a small gain and are not for promotion.
