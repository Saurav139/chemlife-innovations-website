Microencapsulation for Rumen-Sustained and Bypass Delivery

Precision intestinal and bypass release systems

A cow's rumen breaks down much of what it eats before the nutrients reach the intestine. Our multi-layer microencapsulation sets where a nutrient is released: slowly in the rumen, or protected past it to the intestine.

The process

How it works

Each pearl is built so its coat gives way at the right point in the cow's digestive tract.

  1. Step 1

    Build the pearl

    Each pearl has the active nutrient at its core, wrapped in several coating layers. The material and thickness of each layer decide when it gives way.

  2. Step 2

    Choose the release

    For bypass delivery, the coat survives the rumen's microbes intact. For rumen-sustained delivery, it lets the nutrient out gradually, giving rumen microbes a steady supply rather than a single spike.

  3. Step 3

    Release on target

    In the acidic abomasum and then the small intestine, the remaining layers open and the nutrient is released for absorption.

The benefits

Why it matters

  • More reaches the cow

    Protection from rumen breakdown means less of the nutrient is lost before it can be absorbed.

  • A steady supply

    Sustained release avoids peaks and troughs in what the rumen receives.

  • Stable in the bag

    The coating also protects the active through storage and feed processing.
